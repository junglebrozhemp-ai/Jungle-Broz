The Story

Two brothers, one greenhouse, zero shortcuts.

Jungle Broz started as a single hoop-house behind our family's farm. We were tired of hemp flower that showed up dry, over-sprayed, or nothing like the lab sheet promised. So we started growing our own — slow, sun-fed, and tested at every stage from clone to cure.

Today every batch is hand-trimmed, hand-jarred, registered with FDACS, and signed off by our own noses before it ever reaches yours. No shortcuts, no fillers, no mystery trim.

Florida Compliance

Built for Florida's hemp rules, not around them

Jungle Broz operates under the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (FDACS) hemp program. Every product listed on this site is produced and labeled to meet the state's consumable hemp requirements — not just the federal minimum.

This is general information about how we operate, not legal advice. Rules can change between legislative sessions — always check the current requirements at fdacs.gov before purchasing, reselling, or shipping hemp products in Florida.
